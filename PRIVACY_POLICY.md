# Privacy Policy for WhereDidItGo

**Effective Date**: August 20, 2026

WhereDidItGo ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your personal financial privacy. This Privacy Policy outlines how your information is handled when you use the WhereDidItGo application ("App").

---

## 1. 100% Local-First Data Storage

WhereDidItGo is designed from the ground up as a **local-first application**:
* **All Financial Records**: Your income, expenses, account balances, savings goals, category budgets, debts, and transaction notes are stored exclusively on your physical device using local IndexedDB storage.
* **No External Servers**: We do not own, maintain, or transmit your financial data to any remote backend database, server, or cloud storage system.
* **No Account Required**: You do not need to register an account, log in, or provide an email address to use the App.

---

## 2. In-App Purchases & Billing

If you choose to purchase WhereDidItGo Pro:
* Payments are processed directly through **Google Play Billing**.
* We do not collect, process, or store credit card numbers, banking details, or billing addresses.
* Your Pro purchase status is cached locally on your device to verify feature access.

---

## 3. Data Safety & Telemetry

* **Zero Financial Data Transmitted**: Your personal financial data NEVER leaves your device under any circumstances.
* **Optional Diagnostics**: Anonymous app-open and feature-usage telemetry may be collected solely to diagnose app stability, crash rates, and performance performance. No financial amounts, category names, or transaction notes are ever included.

---

## 4. Backups and Data Control

* **Full Data Export**: You can export your data at any time as a JSON backup or CSV spreadsheet.
* **Data Erasure**: You can delete all local application data permanently at any time through **Settings → Reset Data**. Deleting the App from your device permanently removes all stored data.
